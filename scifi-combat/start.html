The battlefield

Take every point of the sector ahead to move the front; break through to their HQ, run them out of reinforcements, or hold more of the line when time runs out. Your squad follows you and covers you when you stop; ping to send it. The city's civilians hide from the fighting: shooting one who isn't shooting at you costs you. Gangs hold their turf against both sides.

Graphics

Runs offline in Chrome, Edge or Firefox (WebGL 2). Bigger battles want a stronger PC: start at 32v32 and Low graphics if it stutters. The full settings (render scale, shadows, every effect, field of view, audio, sensitivity) are the same as the game's Esc menu.
