Frontline · take the points in front of your line; holding all of a sector moves the front. Trenches: stand to fire over the sandbags, crouch for cover, climb out to go over the top; wire slows you. Hold H to dig in (foxhole, sandbags, then a trench to the next position). Your squad follows you and covers you when you stop. Guns (12v12 and up): X to crew one, H to set it up or pack it up; at a howitzer or rocket battery the mouse moves the fire mission's mark, hold LMB to fire. Supply trucks: drive into the HQ dump and stop to load, into the forward depot to unload; H hitches a gun behind you.
Click to lock the mouse · WASD · Shift sprint · C crouch (hold: prone, sprinting: slide) · Alt dive · Space climb/hop · LMB fire · RMB aim · R reload · 1 sword/gun · Q E G Z abilities · X vehicles / hold X by a downed teammate to revive · when downed: crawl, hold Space to give up · Space to deploy when dead.
Stratagems: hold Tab (or Ctrl) and type the code with WASD or the arrows, LMB throws the beacon, Tab again puts it away; stand clear of a red beam. X takes supplies from a pod. Pad: hold LB, the D-pad, RT. Battle points buy extra call-ins, vehicles and the hero from the same panel.
Melee: V drives your rifle butt into whoever's in your face; hold X over a downed enemy to finish him; chevrons round your crosshair are swings coming at you (red: unblockable).
Support: 3 uses your item (medics: a stim for the teammate in front of you, or yourself; engineers: an ammo pack at your feet); engineers hold H beside a damaged vehicle or turret of ours to repair it. Every class has a passive (on your status panel).
