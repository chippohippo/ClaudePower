# Screenshots

Captures from building the game: headless Chromium, software GL (`scripts/art-shots.mjs` for the
art lab, the `.scratch/*-game.mjs` scripts for matches).

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `before-art/` | The first look, before the art direction: the arena duel, the rig lab's dive, a trench going over the top, artillery and fire control, the 400-soldier Extreme Warfront |
| `art/` | The art direction in the art lab (`art-lab.html`): the palette, the scorched sky and ground, both armies' troopers by role, helmets and capes, the vehicles and guns, a composed front-line scene |
| `game/` | The same front-line moments as `before-art/`, in a match with the art direction |
| `stratagems/` | Call-ins (docs/FRONTLINE.md): the wrist pad and its panel, every beam colour at once, a strike's beam and danger fence, the round coming down, the strafing jet, supply and sentry pods, smoke, a barrage, and the same in a real match |
| `feedback/` | Hit feedback and reloads: impacts and bullet holes by surface, a muzzle flash, damage-direction wedges, the friendly-fire marker, the magazine pips |
| `bots/` | Bots that talk: a squadmate's callout subtitled in a match ("Eyes right, contact!"), the squad panel with names |
| `melee/` | Melee on the front (art lab and a match): every class's blade in guard, a duel mid-swing, a spear's thrust, the rifle-butt shove, a finisher, and the windup chevron in a match |
| `support/` | Support roles in a match: a Void medic's passive and stims on the status panel, the stim prompt on a hurt squadmate and the heal it pays, an engineer's ammo pack on the ground |
| `battle-points/` | Battle points: the call-in panel with prices, the "+100 TAKEDOWN" lines, a tank coming down in its drop cradle and the cradle lifting away (art lab), a recon vehicle bought in a match |
| `cars/` | The vehicle overhaul: both recon cars crewed (art lab `?station=cars`), a handbrake slide kicking up dust, a sideways slide tripping on a kerb, the crew flung out as it rolls, the car on its roof, a killed car burning; and in a match, driving with the squad aboard and on the handbrake |
| `city/` | The night city warfront (`index.html?map=city`; art lab `?station=city`): down an avenue under the aurora, a squad under a street lamp and behind a wreck, a burning wreck, the park's fountain on its point, the aurora over the skyline, a barricade line, the city from above; and in a match, at the spawn under a lamp and up at the front facing the enemy's points |
| `falls/` | Hit reactions and the ragdoll's muscles (art lab `?station=falls`): rocked by a hit, a leg buckling, a dying man folding at the knees, a downed one catching his fall, flailing in the air after a blast, writhing on his back, scooting away on his back facing the threat, crawling on his belly, a line of five shot one after another; and side-view filmstrips of the falls from the sim (`.scratch/drive-film.mjs`) |
| `dusk/` | Snowfall at Dusk, the city's stylized look (`index.html?map=city`; art lab `?station=city`): snow lying on every top and falling in big flakes, lavender light, ink outlines, troopers in winter whites; a burning wreck's faceted smoke; and in a match, a shell's chunky smoke at the front and laser bolts crossing the street; rim light on the troopers in their faction's colour (`rim-*`) |
| `cyber/` | The dusk city made futuristic (`city.future`): the skyline of megatowers in neon trim with giant ad screens and a flying car's lights, blade signs, lit shopfronts and cables over a barricaded street, a neon-trimmed crossing, the city from above |
| `vertical/` | The city off the street (art lab `?station=city`, free camera): a house you can go into (real windows, lit rooms, a balcony), scaffolding up a tower front (decks, flights of open treads), a night market (lantern-lit stalls under bright canopies trimmed in neon), a skybridge between two houses |
| `people/` | The city's people (art lab `?station=people`): civilians in street clothes (coats, jackets, hoods, caps, lit glasses, neutral rim), two gangs' raiders in their gang colours |
| `collapse/` | Buildings that come apart (`src/world/Structures.js`), the first version (scripted falls, since replaced by `dynamism/`): a five-storey house, one howitzer round at the foot of its narrow front, the front coming down as one piece (dust hidden to show it), the dust rolling across the street, and after: the floors standing open over the rubble in the street |
| `dynamism/` | Buildings coming apart with physics (`src/physics/RigidWorld.js`, in the style of The Finals' Dynamism), in a match in the city, seen first-person from across the street; the shell's own smoke left out, the collapse's dust in: a five-storey house before; one howitzer round has blown out its two lower storeys, and the three above hang on over the gap (2.6 s); the hanging front tearing loose, pieces tipping off (3.1 s, dust hidden to show it); coming down in chunks, dust rolling out (3.4 s); after: every floor standing open, the front lying in slabs and chunks across the street (8 s) |
| `command/` | The commander's seat (start menu: Commander; `src/ui/CommandView.js`), 12v12 on the trenches: the front from above at the start (both front lines dashed, the points, each squad's marker with its number and men up and a line to where it's going, the squad cards, the order bar, ground nobody's watched darkened); orders given (Anvil sent to take B1 by right-click, its line white for a person's order; Rivet's scout on his way to B2; a fire mission armed over B2, blind, so drawn wide and dashed; the radio acknowledging); half a minute later (the scout on watch, his range drawn and the fog lifted there, two enemies he sees in red, the radio reporting them); zoomed out over the whole map |
| `scopes/` | Looking through a scope (`src/ui/ScopeView.js`): the marksman's Longwatch lowered in the dusk city, then up: the street magnified about 4x in a round sight picture with posts, a fine cross with stadia dots and a lit dot |
| `visibility/` | The city's walls in the shade and at night (`CityRenderer`, `palette.json`): the same street at dusk before (walls in the shade pure black, only their windows showing) and after (lighter facades, dark glass instead of holes, the city's own glow on its walls, more fill and bounce light: the walls read), and the same at night, before and after, and a street at night after |
| `dust/` | What feet and falls kick up (`src/vfx/FootFx.js`), just after a heavy landing, three sprint steps and a dive landing a few metres ahead: the planet's ochre dust on the trenches, snow powder in the city |
| `dives/` | Going over an edge (rig lab stations `diveDrop` and `rollDrop`, side view; then in the dusk city): a sprint dive off a 4 m drop, the body going limp braced for it (arms out ahead and down, the nose dipping as it falls, the hands reaching the ground first), face down, pushed up and up again; a slide off a 3 m ledge coming down crouched, tucking, over the back and up into a crouch; in the game, a roll seen from behind and a braced fall coming down |
| `cover/` | Cover (`T`; `src/movement/CoverFaces.js`, MovementController's `cover` mode) in the dusk city: crouched in behind a wreck in the street, the gun down, the prompt saying how to come out; aiming (`RMB`), up over it with the gun on the street beyond |
| `towers/` | A tower coming down in the dusk city (`Structures.bringDown`), 12v12, seen from down the street: standing (with its ad screen) as it starts to groan; gone from its place, leaning over as a stack of storeys (its facade on its pieces, lights out); breaking up as it falls; crashing down in a cloud of dust; the tower behind it hit and coming down in its turn; the pile it all leaves |
