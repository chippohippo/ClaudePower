# Screenshots

Captures from building the game: headless Chromium, software GL (`scripts/art-shots.mjs` for the
art lab, the `.scratch/*-game.mjs` scripts for matches).

| Folder | What |
|---|---|
| `before-art/` | The first look, before the art direction: the arena duel, the rig lab's dive, a trench going over the top, artillery and fire control, the 400-soldier Extreme Warfront |
| `art/` | The art direction in the art lab (`art-lab.html`): the palette, the scorched sky and ground, both armies' troopers by role, helmets and capes, the vehicles and guns, a composed front-line scene |
| `game/` | The same front-line moments as `before-art/`, in a match with the art direction |
| `stratagems/` | Call-ins (docs/FRONTLINE.md): the wrist pad and its panel, every beam colour at once, a strike's beam and danger fence, the round coming down, the strafing jet, supply and sentry pods, smoke, a barrage, and the same in a real match |
| `feedback/` | Hit feedback and reloads: impacts and bullet holes by surface, a muzzle flash, damage-direction wedges, the friendly-fire marker, the magazine pips |
| `support/` | Support roles in a match: a Void medic's passive and stims on the status panel, the stim prompt on a hurt squadmate and the heal it pays, an engineer's ammo pack on the ground |
| `battle-points/` | Battle points: the call-in panel with prices, the "+100 TAKEDOWN" lines, a tank coming down in its drop cradle and the cradle lifting away (art lab), a recon vehicle bought in a match |
