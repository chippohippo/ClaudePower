UNBROKEN AGES — QUICK START
===========================

One people. Every age. A turn-based 4X strategy game: found a civilization, grow it from the Ancient
era to the far future, and win by Score, Hegemony, Culture, Spiritual, Concord, Exodus or Ascension.

STARTING A GAME
---------------
1. Launch "Unbroken Ages" from the desktop or Start menu.
2. In the lobby choose Single player, pick a nation and leader (the dossier shows how each plays),
   then press Launch. Recommended first game: Small map, 3 AI players, Egypt, Rome or England.
3. Your Settler is selected: press B to found your capital.
4. The advisor at the top teaches the first game step by step. The big button at the bottom right
   always names the next thing that needs you, and becomes NEXT TURN when nothing does.

CONTROLS
--------
Select a unit or city ............ Left-click (click a city again to cycle units inside)
Move or attack ................... Right-click a tile (hover an enemy for the combat preview)
Found city / Fortify ............. B / F
Skip a unit this turn ............ Space
End the turn ..................... Enter, or the turn button
Research and civics .............. T
Diplomacy, city-states, Congress . P
Society (government, faith...) ... G
Espionage ........................ E
Chronicle / Divergence ........... C / V
Supply lens / Resource lens ...... L / R
Yield lens ....................... Y
Quick save / Quick load .......... F5 / F9
Pan / Zoom / Close a screen ...... WASD or arrows / mouse wheel / Esc
Game menu (save, load, audio) .... Esc

SAVES, SETTINGS AND LOGS
------------------------
%APPDATA%\Godot\app_userdata\Unbroken Ages\
  saves\       your saved games (kept when you uninstall)
  logs\        godot.log, useful if something goes wrong
The Start menu folder has a "Saves and Logs" shortcut that opens it.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
* Black screen, crash on start, or very slow on a laptop: use "Unbroken Ages (Safe Mode)" from the
  Start menu. It uses the OpenGL renderer, which works on older and integrated graphics.
* Update your graphics driver if neither works; the game needs Vulkan, DirectX 12 or OpenGL 3.3.
* Multiplayer (Direct IP): the host uses TCP port 47620. Let the game through Windows Firewall
  (the installer can do this) and forward the port on your router for games over the internet.
* Windows SmartScreen may warn that the installer is from an unknown publisher because it is not
  code-signed. Choose "More info" then "Run anyway".
