# Decisions
- **Sim date = integer day index from 1 Jan 1936** (src/sim/date.ts). Alt: JS Date. Why: determinism, cheap compare, trivially serialisable.
- **Modifier scopes as strings ("c:3", "p:12", "g")**. Alt: tagged objects. Why: simple keys for base lookup and filtering.
- **fflate for gzip** instead of pako. Why: smaller, sync API in both Node and browser.
- **Command registry keyed by type string**; systems register handlers at import. Why: AI, UI, console share one path.
- **Provinces = k-means clusters of Natural Earth admin-1 regions**, allocated per (owner, continent), Europe ×30 weight, majors' homelands ≥ 8. Alt: hand-drawn. Why: generated, reproducible, real borders.
- **1936 ownership = modern country → owner table + admin-1 overrides** (data/map_1936.json). Why: no clean free 1936 admin-1 dataset.
- **Colonies belong to the metropole** for the slice. Revisit with diplomacy.
- **Terrain/dev from lat-lon boxes** (scripts/map-rules.ts). Why: no extra dataset needed.
- **Map renders on demand** (Pixi autoStart off). Why: static map, zero idle cost.
- **Worker owns the game clock**; speeds = [2,5,10,30,120] days/sec. Speed 3 ≈ 1 month / 3 s.
- **Starting industry sized from 1936 demand** (build-pops input–output pass) rather than headcount guesses. Why: headcount sizing left supply at 20% of demand and the economy collapsed.
- **Wages = 65% of building value added**, profits → elites (dividends) → investment pool. Why: wages track productivity with no separate wage model; money stays closed.
- **Markets: national clear, then a world market on residuals** at a world price. Alt: bilateral trade routes. Why: simple, lets resource-poor countries import; routes/blocs later.
- **Monetary policy mints seigniorage when consumer prices fall below target.** Why: real output grows ~7× over the century; a fixed money stock deflated into collapse. Printing beyond the debt limit drives the inflation channel instead.
- **Demography applied monthly at yearly rates /12**, in its own monthly slot after construction (documented in tick.ts).
- **Mobility, migration, assimilation via transferPop** (moves people, cash, averaged traits); small groups merge yearly (≤40/province).
- **Exogenous tech placeholders** (productivity, medicine, schooling, PM unlock years) until the tech system (M7).
- **Commands apply immediately between ticks** (not queued to the next day); undo = restore a save snapshot taken before the command, valid until time advances (depth 10).
- **Laws are modifier bundles** (data/laws.json, source "law:<category>"); systems read rates with countryStat() (fast) and tooltips use getStat() (breakdown).
- **Development split into tax/production/manpower** (dev = sum). Tax dev raises per-province tax efficiency, production dev counts double toward productivity, manpower dev raises manpower. No infrastructure cap yet; diminishing returns come from the productivity cap.
- **Automation flags per country** (develop/build/budget). AI countries run with all on; the player can take over each system separately.
- **Map repaints only when a colour changes**; previews refresh per game month and after every command (snapshot.rev).
- **Interim point gains +5 (ADM/DIP/MIL) and PP +4** until leaders and IGs supply them (M4).
- **IG clout = Σ pop political weight (size × √relative wealth × (0.3+literacy)) × stratum attraction × IG power** (Armed Forces ×8: guns beyond headcount).
- **Approval target = laws + own privilege + rivals' privileges + members' SoL + representation + head's ideology + fading favour + unmet demand** (IGs above 15% clout demand their privilege). Smoothed 8%/month; radicalism follows anger × size.
- **Strikes** when radicalism > 40 and clout > 8%: unrest in members' provinces (output −3%/point via unrest).
- **Elections** every 4 years (democracies): clout × approval × ideology fit; the winning IG's leader governs. **Coups** when Armed Forces radicalism > 60 (75 in democracies) and clout > 10%.
- **Law cost rises with opposition** (×1 + 1.5×clout-weighted opposition); opponents gain radicalism. Preview shows the support bar.
- **Characters**: Gompertz mortality, succession via heir → best minister → generated; auto-advisors fill seats. Portraits are deterministic SVG from a seed.
- **Resource flags use province centroids**, so boxes must cover cluster centroids (US Gulf/California oil widened).
- **One JSON scripting language for focuses, events and ambitions** (conditions + effects, each with a plain-language describer for previews). Content compiled into data/generated/content.json so Node, tests and the browser load the same bundle.
- **Focus layout is computed** (depth = longest prerequisite chain, x by branch) at build time.
- **Generic modular trees are concatenated** into one "National Development" tree for nations without their own.
- **Events fire on sim state with mean-time-to-happen**; AI takes the highest-weight choice; the player's events wait (game auto-pauses). Province/IG events bind a matching province/IG for templates.
- **Consistency linter** checks references, flags read-but-never-set / set-but-undeclared, focus graph errors, unreachable focuses, and that every major flag has reaction content.
- **Reachability harness** proves each focus by completing its prerequisite path on a cloned world while forcing conditions (flags, stats, laws, IG values).
- **Armies are made of military pops** (recruit moves peasants/workers into the military stratum; casualties are deaths in the ledger; demobilisation sends them back). Equipment = share of arms needs actually bought on the market.
- **Combat**: power = men × equipment × organisation × general × terrain × entrenchment × industry/doctrine; daily losses ∝ enemy power / own power, capped at 3%/day (no instant wipes); retreat at organisation < 25%. Every factor is in the battle report.
- **War score** from occupied value (development + population) ×1.5, battle results (±25) and holding the war goal; peace terms cost score; AI takes what its score buys after 3 months or when the loser is exhausted.
- **War AI** needs a casus belli (claims, independence, ideological hostility), relations < −40, coalition power edge 1.6×, aggression (ideology, mobilisation, Armed Forces, militarist flags; democracies ×0.3) and a 2.5%×aggression monthly roll. Nuclear deterrence blocks wars against nuclear powers.
- **Printing capped at 2% of money per month**; unpaid bills cost stability (stops endless hyperinflation after lost wars).
- **Soldiers don't count as urban.**
- M7: Tech is an effective year per tree (not discrete research picks). Growth uses relYear = min(year + 1936 lag, max(year, calendar)). This keeps the 1936 calibration (everyone starts at "1936" growth) while laggards fall behind and catch up by diffusion.
- M7: Tech modifiers avoid investment and construction bonuses, which double-counted productivity growth and pushed urbanisation past 65%; those techs give tax efficiency instead. Idea bonuses are halved.
- M7: Era goods are deferred to M8 balance; production-method unlocks already follow industry tech.
