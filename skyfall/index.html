Slide: sprint then crouch. Boost has a 2s cooldown.

Slide jump / slide hop: jump out of a slide and hold crouch as you land to chain slides with no speed loss.

Bunny hop: jump on the exact landing frame — friction is skipped.

Tap strafe / air strafe: in the air, tap a strafe key while turning the mouse to redirect momentum.

Wall bounce: press jump just as you touch a wall in the air.

Climb / mantle: jump into a wall and hold W; you'll climb then mantle any ledge.

Superglide: at the very end of a mantle, press jump + crouch on the same frame.

Skydive: look down to dive faster, look level to glide further.
